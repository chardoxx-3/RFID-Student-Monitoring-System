Email Templates Guide
Using Dynamic Variables

Variables allow you to personalize emails. They are automatically replaced with real data when an email is sent.

Template Example:
Subject: Attendance - {student_name}

Dear {guardian_name},
Your child {student_name} from {section} has arrived at school at {time_in}.
Result:
Subject: Attendance - John Doe

Dear Mrs. Smith,
Your child John Doe from BSIT-1A has arrived at school at 07:45 AM.
Available Variables
  • {student_name} - Full name of student
  • {guardian_name} - Parent/Guardian name
  • {time_in} - Check-in time
  • {time_out} - Check-out time
  • {date} - Date of attendance
  • {section} - Student's section/class
Tips
  • Keep subject lines concise.
  • Verify SMTP settings if emails aren't sending.
  • Use the "Send Test Email" feature to verify your layout.
